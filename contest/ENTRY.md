## Short description

The lighthouse has gone still. Choose a companion and follow the lost rhythm of Asterra in a creature-collecting RPG you can play in your browser.

## Entry description

Your first assignment as a field researcher takes you from the Brinewick meadow to a celestial observatory. Find creatures by following their tracks. Tune the chimes on an old woodland trail and see what answers. Earn eight League badges to discover why the Stilllight Society is trying to hold the region’s Wildlight steady.

Build a party of six from 300 collectible species. Each opponent signals its next move, so a switch at the right moment can turn a resisted hit into the energy for a stronger reply. Solve eight gym puzzles, explore ten legendary sanctuaries and enter the Asterra Championship after the story.

Wildlight is free and plays in a modern browser with keyboard, mouse or touch. It saves locally and includes a portable backup. Open in-game Settings to turn on the soundtrack, change text speed or reduce motion.

## How Wildlight uses SHIFT

In battle, the foe signals its next move. Shift to a partner that resists that attack and the hit charges a relay. The incoming partner gains three energy and 25% power on its next successful strike. Build a team whose resistances give you reasons to trade places.

Out on the trails, the Stilllight Society has locked Asterra’s natural cycles in place. Chimes respond to the order of rain, tide or light. Eventually you help the observatory’s rings move at different speeds again.

## First expedition

1. Start a new expedition and follow the gold marker to Oriel. Preview the three partners before choosing one.
2. Meet Gravlet in the west meadow. Strike to lower its health, then use a Lumen Capsule once the command shows a sure catch.
3. Take the east trail. Read the notebook at the fork and follow the three clues to reveal a Spinchime.
4. Reach Briarbrook and enter Glassleaf House. Turn the greenhouse valves, meet Mim and challenge Fern. Read the foe’s intent and look for a companion marked **Relay on hit** in the Switch menu.

Move with WASD, arrow keys or a click on the ground. Use **E** or **Enter** to interact, **Esc** to open the menu, **M** for the map and **Tab** for the party. Touch controls appear on phones and tablets.
